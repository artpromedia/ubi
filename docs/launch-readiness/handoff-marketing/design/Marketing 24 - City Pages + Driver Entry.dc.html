Drivers · Lagos

Drive with UBI in Lagos

Apply in the UBI Driver app. You upload your documents once, watch each one get reviewed, and go online when everything is verified. Your share of every fare is shown on the trip and on your weekly statement.

Apply in the UBI Driver app Already applied? Open the app

Applying is free. We never ask for payment to review documents.

What you'll need in Lagos

The exact list comes from UBI's verification rules for Lagos and is repeated inside the app.

  • 1Driver's licence. Valid, in your name
  • 2LASDRI card. Lagos State Drivers' Institute
  • 3Identity. NIN, plus a selfie check in the app
  • 4Vehicle papers. Registration, roadworthiness, third-party insurance
  • 5Background check. You consent in the app; UBI runs it
  • 6A car for Go, Comfort or XL. Class eligibility is confirmed from your vehicle details in the app

Own several cars? Fleet arrangements are agreed with UBI directly.

What happens next

  1. 01
    Create your account
    Phone number and a one-time code. No password to remember.
  2. 02
    Add your vehicle
    Make, model, year, plate. The app tells you which classes it qualifies for.
  3. 03
    Upload documents
    Photograph each one in the app. Each shows Under review, Verified, or Needs attention with the reason.
  4. 04
    Selfie check
    A short liveness check matches you to your ID. Repeated occasionally to keep accounts safe.
  5. 05
    Go online
    Once everything is verified you can take trips. Set your own hours.

Review times vary with volume; the app shows the live status of each document rather than a promised date.

How your pay is shown

  • Every trip shows the fare, UBI's service fee (20% of the fare in Lagos) and what you keep. Before you accept the next one.
  • Tips and tolls are yours; they're listed separately and never commissioned.
  • Cash trips: you keep the cash; what you owe UBI nets against your wallet on the weekly statement.
  • Any incentive UBI runs appears as its own line with its rules. We don't advertise earnings here because they depend on when and where you drive.

Safety works both ways

  • Riders confirm the trip with a PIN, so you know you have the right passenger.
  • Emergency 112 and UBI Support are one tap away from the trip screen.
  • Cancellations by riders after you're assigned are charged to them, not to you.
