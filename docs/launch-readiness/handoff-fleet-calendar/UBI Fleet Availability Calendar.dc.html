Design A05 · Fleet availability & maintenance calendar · 23 Sep 2026

Fleets plan vehicles. Drivers own their time. Riders keep their trip.

Screen-flow handoff for the fleet calendar: availability model, fleet portal (Next.js, dark), driver app (React Native, dark) and the rider moments it causes. The fleet backend is not built and the fleet flag is off — every screen here is a design reference.

{{ icons.alert }} Fixture data. Every plate, driver name, time and amount on this board is invented to show layout and states. None is real, and none should be copied into seed data.
Fleets propose, drivers consent
No fleet action commits a driver's time, bids, or earnings. Any change to terms needs a new PIN signature.
Bookings are never silently moved
Maintenance cannot cancel a booking. Changing the driver or vehicle needs the rider's consent.
Fleets see opaque blocks
"Booked · 07:15–09:40". No rider, address, route or safety evidence — ever.
Server decides feasibility
Conflicts, travel time and money come from the backend. Screens show "Checking…", never assume success.
Calendar layers Signed shift Proposed (not counted) Booked (opaque) Maintenance Held by UBI Driver availability Driver time off Conflict Every block also carries a text label, so colour is never the only signal.
